Updated spell handling for characters and custom objects, more subclasses and features should auto prepare their spells and adding spells to characters is easier

Added dice analyzer and matchup game tools

Added the Homebrew Studio game tool: write homebrew with reference panels beside it, find existing content that does the same thing and check a monster's numbers against its CR

The monster editor now works out proficiency, hit die, hit points, saves, skills and attacks from the stats (hover a number to see why) and has folding sections; the class and subclass editors have a section ready for every level; the Add buttons on the collection pages open the Homebrew Studio (a setting, on by default)

Studio Insights are stricter: at least 4 matching words, exact matches hidden, unofficial content only when its box is ticked, and anything matching more than 10 things is left out as standard wording (all adjustable in Settings > Loading Options)

Character sheets: AC, hit points, initiative, spell attack and save DC can be hovered to see how they were worked out and typed over by hand (the tooltip then shows "Manually set / Auto detection"), bonuses from magic items, features, feats and species traits are found automatically, and the equipment, magic item and feat pickers have a green Add button on every row like the spell picker (feats are removed by right-clicking them)
