Added the Tabletop game tool: maps with square, hex or no grid, tokens (from a folder, a zip, the bestiary or your characters), condition rings, hit points, hidden tokens, a ruler, drawing, pings and fog of war, shared with players over the LAN. Tokens follow the initiative tracker both ways

Tabletop walls and doors with line of sight, token sight ranges, spell areas in 7 shapes, elevation and height zones. Players can open doors they can see, the DM can lock them, and "Remember explored areas" dims places players have already seen

Players can join a session from a phone or tablet browser: turn on "Let browsers join" on the Session page and scan the QR code. Browser players see the tabletop, chat, dice and turn order

Added the Music game tool: your own music library with tags, playlists and fades

Added the guided Character Builder on the Characters page, with class features (fighting styles, invocations, expertise, weapon masteries) and multiclassing

Collection types can now be edited: add your own fields to the built-in collections or make new collections. A "Your fields" button on every built-in page filters by the fields you added

View and Compare now works for monsters: AC, HP, speed, ability scores and saves, resistances and immunities, senses, CR, damage per round, attack bonus, save DC and legendary actions are coloured better or worse. For spells it shows the total of all the damage dice again, treats dice joined by "or" as a choice, and leaves out higher-level and cantrip upgrade dice

The guided tour covers the new additions

Full-text search across everything, including the Rules pages, and 41 keyboard shortcuts you can change

New game tools: Random Tables (d100 and more, with rolling), a Calendar with your own months and holidays, and a PDF Library with search

Pictures for content from image packs you add in Settings > Pictures, and Export PDF in every collection's Select bar. The Homebrew Studio has a picture slot and can export a draft as a PDF

Settings > Backup and Transfer makes one file with your settings, content, characters, tabletop, pictures, PDFs and music, to move to another computer or keep safe

A guided tour of the app (Settings > Help or F1), and Send feedback (Settings > Help or Shift+F1) to report a bug or suggest an idea

Character sheets update much faster when you change level, subclass, classes or feats, and the Rogue's Slippery Mind no longer causes an error

Fixes: tooltips and drop-downs no longer linger on screen, the Random Tables page scrolls on small windows, and start-up errors (such as a missing package) are now shown instead of the app silently not opening
